Good to Know
What’s the difference between the two shows?
The Card & Collectibles Show is the wider one — sports cards, TCG, comics, toys, memorabilia and collectibles of every kind. The TCG Card Show focuses on trading card games, mainly Pokémon and One Piece. Same venue, same hours, same free admission.
What does it cost to get in?
Nothing — admission is free at both shows. No ticket, no gate fee. All ages welcome, so bring the whole family.
How do I get a vendor table?
Tables are $45 each and we run 75 of them at each show. Call or text Chase at 567-208-0307, or email Info@findlaycardshow.com with which show you want, the date, how many tables, and what you sell.
Is there parking?
Yes — free parking on site at the Hancock County Fairgrounds, with easy load-in for vendors.
Can I bring cards to sell or trade?
Absolutely. Plenty of vendors buy collections and trade with attendees. Bring your binder.
