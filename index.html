Good to Know
What does it cost to get in?
Nothing — admission is free. No ticket, no gate fee. All ages welcome, so bring the whole family.
What kinds of cards will I find?
Pokémon and One Piece are the backbone of the show, alongside other TCGs. Expect singles, sealed product, graded slabs, supplies, and plenty of trading.
How do I get a vendor table?
Tables are $45 each and we run 75 of them. Call or text Chase at 567-208-0307, or email Info@findlaycardshow.com with the show date, how many tables you want, and what you sell.
Is there parking?
Yes — free parking on site at the Hancock County Fairgrounds, with easy load-in for vendors.
Can I bring cards to sell or trade?
Absolutely. Plenty of vendors buy collections and trade with attendees. Bring your binder.
